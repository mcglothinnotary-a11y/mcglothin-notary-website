About McGlothin Notary Services LLC

McGlothin Notary Services LLC provides professional mobile notary services by appointment only throughout Metro Atlanta. We offer convenient, reliable notarization for individuals, families, and businesses, including general documents, real estate signings, hospital visits, and more.

With flexible evening and weekend availability, we bring notary services directly to your location—saving you time and helping ensure a smooth, professional experience.

Serving McDonough, Stockbridge, Jonesboro, Locust Grove, and surrounding Metro Atlanta areas.

  • Loan Signing Services

  • Estate Planning Document Notarization (Wills, Powers of Attorney, Advance Directives)
  • Hospital & Nursing Home Notary Visits
