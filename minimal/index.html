About

McGlothin Notary Services LLC provides professional, reliable, and confidential mobile notary services for individuals and businesses throughout Metro Atlanta and surrounding areas. Services are provided by appointment at the client’s location.

Services

  • Mobile Notary Services
  • Loan Document Notarization (No Legal Advice)
  • Estate Planning Document Notarization
    (Wills, Powers of Attorney, Advance Directives)
  • Hospital & Nursing Home Notary Visits
  • Small Business & Corporate Document Notarization
  • Apostille Coordination Assistance (Third-Party Processing)
  • Same-Day & After-Hours Appointments

All services are limited to notarial acts permitted under Georgia law. McGlothin Notary Services LLC does not provide legal or financial advice.

Service Area

Serving Metro Atlanta and surrounding areas through mobile, appointment-based notary services.
